# SafeCompute: Private Financial Estimations Powered by Fhenix FHE

> **"Keep your financials private. Keep your calculations on-chain."**

Traditional blockchains and AI models suffer from a fundamental transparency trade-off: to verify or compute financial data, it must be decrypted, exposing sensitive user information to validators, front-running bots, and public explorers. Our project breaks this cycle using Fhenix’s Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE). Unlike standard encryption that only protects data at rest or in transit, FHE allows us to perform complex financial logic—like tax or yield calculations—directly on encrypted ciphertexts. The blockchain processes the "encrypted math" without ever knowing the underlying input, ensuring that sensitive data remains cryptographically private throughout the entire lifecycle while remaining fully verifiable.

---

## 💡 The Problem: Blockchain's Privacy Paradox

Web3 promises decentralization, transparency, and trustless execution, but it comes at a severe cost to user privacy:
* **The Financial Doxxing Risk:** If you calculate your taxes, net wealth, or credit score on Ethereum, Sepolia, or any EVM chain today, **every detail is public**. Anyone with your wallet address can inspect the contract storage, view your inputs (e.g., salary, deductions), and track your financial status.
* **Lack of Confidential DeFi Tools:** Because smart contracts cannot natively handle secret data, users are forced to choose between Web2 centralized estimators (which harvest data) or fully transparent Web3 options (which dox them).

---

## 🚀 The Solution: SafeCompute

SafeCompute solves the privacy paradox by introducing **shielded smart contract execution**. 

Using **Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE)** via the Fhenix fhEVM, the app performs mathematical calculations directly on encrypted data.

### 🌟 Key Product Pillars

### 1. Zero-Knowledge-Like Privacy, Simple UX
Users do not need to generate complex cryptographic keys or wait for slow local proofs. They input their financial parameters, and the application handles local encryption and on-chain execution seamlessly.

### 2. Computation on Encrypted State
The blockchain computes variables homomorphically. It handles logical operations (e.g., determining if deductions are greater than income) and arithmetic calculations (e.g., calculating percentage-based tax liabilities and subtracting liabilities from income) without ever decrypting the numbers.

### 3. Signature-Based Self-Sovereign Access (Permits)
Your data is stored on the public ledger in an encrypted format. Only you can view your calculations. Decryption requests are validated using EIP-712 cryptographic signatures (Permits) generated by your connected wallet.

## ⚔️ Why FHE vs. Other Algorithms?

While other privacy techniques exist, they fail to provide the specific combination of computational privacy and on-chain composability that FHE delivers:

* **Vs. ZK-Proofs (Zero-Knowledge):** ZK is built for verification (proving a statement is true), not computation. ZK is excellent for privacy-preserving identity, but it struggles to handle shared state. In our app, if you want to perform cross-user financial analytics (e.g., aggregate pool volume or risk), ZK requires massive coordination. FHE allows us to perform arbitrary operations on shared, encrypted state natively.
* **Vs. TEEs (Trusted Execution Environments):** TEEs rely on hardware-based isolation (like Intel SGX). You must trust the hardware manufacturer and the specific enclave setup. FHE is cryptographically secure—it relies on lattice-based math (which is also quantum-resistant) rather than hardware trust. FHE provides a truly decentralized guarantee that no single entity, including the node operator, can access your raw data.
